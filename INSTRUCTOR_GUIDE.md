# Instructor Guide

Project the simulation and enter one decision per team. The simulation deliberately separates two effects:

1. **Immediate decision effect:** the visible cost or benefit of Invest, Hold Cash, Borrow, or Hedge.
2. **Global shock effect:** the uncertain outcome revealed after all teams decide.

This prevents hedging, borrowing, and liquidity from appearing to be free choices. Pause after each step and ask teams to explain which metric they improved and which metric they sacrificed.

Suggested sequence: opening metrics, team choice, immediate metrics, shock reveal, shock metrics, cumulative ranking, short debrief.

The scoring values are fictional instructional parameters, not forecasts or investment recommendations.
