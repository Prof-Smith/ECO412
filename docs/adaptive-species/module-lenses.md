## Common diagnostic

For every module, use this sequence:

1. **Principle:** What does the benchmark predict?
2. **Dissent:** What does the evidence challenge?
3. **Reversal:** When might the apparent error be adaptive?
4. **Environment:** Which cues, incentives, and constraints shape behavior?
5. **Revision:** What evidence should change the model?
