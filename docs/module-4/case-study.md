# Case Study 3: Rebuilding Trust Without Exploiting It

Select a real offer, service interface, guarantee, subscription, public message, or ordering environment. Produce a three-page audit:

1. Describe the decision environment.
2. Identify the belief or trust problem.
3. Distinguish the observed phenomenon from possible explanations.
4. Explain the information asymmetry or social influence involved.
5. Recommend a low-cost redesign that improves transparency or private commitment.
6. State what evidence would show that the redesign worked.
7. Identify one unintended consequence.

Include one original Plotly figure or decision map.
