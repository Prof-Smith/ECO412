# Module 4 Instructor Guide

## Recommended content placement

Keep the distrust, source-credibility, public-goods, and public-versus-private choice material in Module 4 because each concerns belief formation, heuristics, context, and social influence.

Move the Expected Utility Theory, Allais paradox, prospect theory, reflection effect, probability weighting, and prospect-theory criticism material to Module 5, Decision Making Under Risk and Uncertainty. Those topics form a coherent normative-versus-descriptive risk sequence.

Move the ticket ownership, willingness-to-pay versus willingness-to-accept, endowment effect, loss aversion in ownership, IKEA effect, and trial-ownership examples to Module 6, Mental Accounting and Ownership. The content is valuable, but it will be more coherent when paired with acquisition utility, transaction utility, sunk cost, and mental accounts.

## Additions recommended for Module 4

Add a concise mechanism map covering source credibility, prior experience, availability, confirmation, social proof, reputational utility, and asymmetric information. Keep the emphasis on distinguishing a behavioral phenomenon from an explanation.

## Debrief

Ask whether distrust is irrational when deception is common. Then ask when protective skepticism becomes socially costly by blocking beneficial exchange. For the social-choice study, separate preference change, strategic self-presentation, and simple variety seeking.
