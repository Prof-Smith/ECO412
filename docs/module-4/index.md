## Learning outcomes

By the end of Module 4, students will be able to:

1. Explain how source credibility and prior experience influence belief formation.
2. Distinguish skepticism, distrust, source effects, social influence, and reputational utility.
3. Identify when a heuristic may be protective in one environment and costly in another.
4. Compare private and public preference elicitation.
5. Interpret group differences without treating one individual response as evidence of an effect.
6. Design a transparent intervention that improves decisions without exploiting limited attention or trust.

## Two-week pathway

**Week 7:** belief formation, source effects, distrust, and asymmetric information.  
**Week 8:** public choice, social differentiation, class analysis, and the Behavioral Environment Audit.
