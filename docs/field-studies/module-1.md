## Research question

> How does the visible architecture of one bounded setting appear to make a choice easier to notice, understand, or complete?

## Step 1: Choose a setting

Choose a campus form, menu, pricing display, registration screen, recycling station, library display, queue, sign, or service process. Observe the environment rather than identifiable people.

!!! warning "Privacy boundary"
    Do not collect names, student IDs, faces, identifiable photographs, private records, protected-characteristic data, covert audio, or covert video.

## Step 2: Separate evidence from inference

<div class="m1one-three"><article><small>OBSERVED</small><p>The recommended option appears first and is preselected.</p></article><article><small>INFERRED</small><p>The arrangement may reduce search or completion effort.</p></article><article><small>NOT ESTABLISHED</small><p>The observation does not prove intent or causal impact.</p></article></div>
