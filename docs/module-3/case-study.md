# Case Study 2: The Experience You Bought

## Assignment

Select a consumer experience with a meaningful before, during, and after phase. Examples may include travel, dining, entertainment, education, fitness, subscriptions, or a major purchase.

## Required sections

1. **Predicted utility:** What did the consumer expect?
2. **Decision utility:** Which attributes and reference points shaped the choice?
3. **Instant utility:** How might utility vary during the experience?
4. **Remembered utility:** Which peak and ending are likely to dominate recall?
5. **Transaction utility:** How did the perceived deal differ from product value?
6. **Redesign:** Recommend one transparent intervention that improves experienced utility without exploiting the consumer.
7. **Measurement:** Specify how predicted, instant, and remembered utility would be collected.

## Required visual

Create one original Plotly temporal utility profile containing predicted utility, instant utility, and remembered utility.
