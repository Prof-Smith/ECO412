# Module 2 Instructor Guide

## Teaching purpose

Module 2 shifts students from consuming behavioral findings to evaluating how findings are produced. The central distinction is between an interesting result and credible evidence.

## Suggested debrief sequence

1. **Theory:** Have students defend trade-offs among the six theory criteria rather than assuming every criterion can be maximized simultaneously.
2. **Design:** Ask students to identify the unit of assignment, treatment, comparison, and outcome before discussing results.
3. **Randomization:** Emphasize that random assignment balances groups in expectation, not perfectly in every realized sample.
4. **Evidence:** Separate the observed effect size, uncertainty, statistical decision, and practical importance.
5. **Validity:** Distinguish internal validity from external validity.
6. **Ethics:** Require a research safeguard that is operational, not merely “be ethical.”

## Class extension

Use students' exported JSON files to compare proposed hypotheses and validity concerns. Remove names and identifiers before combining submissions.

## Important boundary

The interactive score is instructional. It is not a validated measure of research competence and should not be used to compare students.
