# Case Study 1: Can We Trust the Result?

## Assignment

Select one behavioral-economics study discussed in the course or approved by the instructor. Produce a three-page research audit.

## Required sections

1. **Claim and theory:** State the behavioral claim and assess all six good-theory criteria.
2. **Design map:** Identify participants, assignment, treatment, comparison, outcome, and timing.
3. **Evidence:** Describe the key pattern without overstating causality or generality.
4. **Validity:** Evaluate one internal-validity threat and one external-validity threat.
5. **Research ethics:** Address incentives, deception, consent, scrutiny, context, stakes, or debriefing as applicable.
6. **Replication:** Propose one study that would increase confidence and explain what result would count against the original claim.

## Required visual

Include one original design diagram or Plotly chart that clarifies the study's logic or evidence.
