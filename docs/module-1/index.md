# Module 1: Human or Econ?

## Testing the Rational Benchmark Against Human Choice

Module 1 introduces behavioral economics by comparing real human decisions with the Standard Economic Model. You will examine rationality, expected-utility maximization, Bayesian updating, self-interest, fungibility, and time-consistent preferences. The objective is not to dismiss conventional economic theory, but to understand what the benchmark predicts, where behavior differs, and what those differences may reveal.

## Readings

- Wilkinson and Klaes, Chapter 1: *Nature of Behavioral Economics*
- Ariely reader: Introduction and *The Truth about Relativity*
- Kahneman reader excerpt on automatic and effortful processing
