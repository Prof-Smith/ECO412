# Case Study 1: Designed Choice

## Assignment

Select one organization, product, public program, or university process that uses context to influence choice. Analyze the design through the Standard Economic Model and behavioral economics.

Your three-page submission should include:

1. **Decision environment:** What choice does the person face?
2. **SEM benchmark:** What would the benchmark predict?
3. **Behavioral mechanism:** Identify and explain one mechanism demonstrated in Module 1.
4. **Evidence:** Use credible evidence rather than assuming the mechanism works.
5. **Value creation:** Explain who benefits and how.
6. **Responsible stewardship:** Identify transparency, autonomy, fairness, or welfare concerns.
7. **Redesign:** Recommend one improvement and define how its effect could be tested.

## Suggested contexts

Pricing menus, subscription plans, donation forms, retirement enrollment, food ordering, financial applications, streaming interfaces, or course registration.

## Required visual

Include one original figure showing the decision architecture or predicted choice paths. The figure may be a diagram, table, or Plotly chart.
