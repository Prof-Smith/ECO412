## Before writing

- Complete the Human or Econ? Lab and export the lab record.
- Complete the Choice Architecture Walk and export the field record.
- Use the Adaptive Species Planning Guide.
- Select one decision environment for analysis.

## Assignment

Analyze one organization, product, public program, or university process that uses context to influence choice. The case study should be **1,000–1,250 words**, excluding references and the required visual.

## Required sections

1. **Decision environment**: Define the choice, options, setting, and relevant constraints.
2. **SEM benchmark**: State the relevant assumption and prediction.
3. **Observed evidence**: Use at least one lab result and one Choice Architecture Walk observation.
4. **Behavioral mechanism**: Explain the strongest Module 1 account while separating phenomenon from mechanism.
5. **Alternative explanation**: Present a credible operational, informational, or nonbehavioral account.
6. **Adaptive Species analysis**: Explain possible function, environmental fit, hidden cost, and mismatch.
7. **Responsible stewardship and redesign**: Address transparency, autonomy, fairness, or welfare and recommend one proportionate improvement.
8. **Evaluation and revision trigger**: Define a comparison, outcome, and evidence that would change the diagnosis.

## Required visual

Include one original, de-identified figure showing the decision architecture, evidence, predicted choice path, or proposed redesign. Add a title and source note, and discuss the figure in the text.
