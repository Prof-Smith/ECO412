## Assignment

Select one organization, product, public program, or university process that uses context to influence choice. Analyze the design through the Standard Economic Model and behavioral economics.

Your submission should be **1,000–1,250 words**, excluding references and the required visual.

## Required sections

1. **Decision environment**  
   What choice does the decision-maker face? Identify the options, relevant constraints, and setting.

2. **SEM benchmark**  
   State the relevant Standard Economic Model assumption and its prediction.

3. **Observed evidence**  
   Use at least one result from the Human or Econ? lab and one observation from the Choice Architecture Walk. Describe what the evidence shows without overstating causality.

4. **Behavioral mechanism**  
   Identify and explain one primary Module 1 mechanism. Distinguish the observed phenomenon from the proposed explanation.

5. **Alternative explanation**  
   Present one credible operational, informational, or nonbehavioral account that could also explain the design or pattern.

6. **Adaptive Species analysis**  
   Explain the possible function of the shortcut or environmental feature, the conditions under which it may be useful, and where the fit may become a mismatch.

7. **Responsible stewardship and redesign**  
   Identify transparency, autonomy, fairness, or welfare concerns. Recommend one proportionate improvement.

8. **Evaluation and revision trigger**  
   Explain how the redesign could be evaluated and state the evidence that would weaken or change the diagnosis.

## Required visual

Include one original figure showing the decision architecture or predicted choice path. The visual may be:

- An annotated screenshot with identifying information removed
- A decision-path diagram
- A comparison table
- A chart created from the de-identified lab record
- A before-and-after redesign mockup

The figure must include a title, source note, and a brief explanation in the text.

## Submission package

Upload the following to **SUBMIT • Module 1 Model Diagnostic Case Study** in Canvas:

- Case study as `.docx` or `.pdf`
- Required visual included in the document
- Lab record as a supporting file
- Choice Architecture Walk record as a supporting file

## Pre-submission check

- The SEM benchmark is accurate and specific.
- Lab and field evidence are both used.
- Observation is separated from inferred mechanism.
- A credible alternative explanation is considered.
- Adaptive benefit and mismatch are both discussed.
- The redesign preserves autonomy and transparency.
- A genuine revision trigger is stated.
