## Learning outcomes

By the end of Module 6, students will be able to:

1. Explain mental accounting as the coding, categorization, and evaluation of economic outcomes.
2. Apply reference dependence to integration and segregation of gains and losses.
3. Distinguish acquisition utility from transaction utility.
4. Explain choice bracketing, payment coupling, and payment depreciation.
5. Diagnose sunk-cost persistence without assuming that every continuation decision is irrational.
6. Compare willingness to pay and willingness to accept in ownership settings.
7. Design a budgeting or pricing architecture that uses mental accounts transparently.

## Two-week pathway

**Week 11:** account labels, fungibility, hedonic editing, transaction utility, and reference prices.  
**Week 12:** sunk cost, ownership, payment timing, applied finance, and the Mental Accounting Design Brief.
