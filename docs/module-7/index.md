## Learning outcomes

1. Identify players, strategies, information, beliefs, incentives, and payoffs.
2. Distinguish dominant-strategy games, coordination games, social dilemmas, and bargaining games.
3. Explain how fairness, reciprocity, trust, guilt, reputation, and social norms alter predictions.
4. Compare one-shot and repeated interaction.
5. Diagnose information cascades, focal points, and strategic uncertainty.
6. Evaluate when individually adaptive behavior creates collectively weak outcomes.
7. Recommend a low-cost change to incentives, information, timing, or commitment.

## Two-week pathway

**Week 13:** strategic interaction, social dilemmas, trust, bargaining, and coordination.  
**Week 14:** repeated games, reputation, norm enforcement, institutional design, and the Game Redesign Memo.
