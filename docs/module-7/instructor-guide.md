# Module 7 Instructor Guide

## Behavioral game theory expansion

Add the social-norm and market-norm material moved from Module 6 here, but frame it as norm activation and incentive crowding rather than mental accounting. Use the daycare fine as a game redesign that changed the meaning of lateness, not merely its price.

Add trust games, ultimatum games, public-goods dilemmas, reciprocity, inequity aversion, guilt aversion, focal points, information cascades, reputation, and repeated cooperation. The purpose is not to survey every model. Students should learn to identify which behavioral assumption changes the strategic prediction.

## Adaptive Species connection

This module directly supports the book's claim that humans adapt to other adaptive agents. Emphasize that poor collective outcomes can emerge from sensible local adaptation to weak incentives, information, or norms.

## Recommended boundary

Do not make cooperation a moral score. The same action can reflect trust, risk, fairness, strategic calculation, fear of exploitation, or a response to the institution.
