# Capstone Project Brief

## Behavioral Insights Project

Select a real decision environment that is accessible, ethically appropriate, and narrow enough to analyze. Examples include a campus process, student service, financial decision interface, subscription, budgeting tool, public message, team process, consumer setting, or digital choice environment.

## Required deliverables

1. **Executive brief**, 1,500–2,000 words.
2. **Behavioral diagnosis map** showing environment, mechanism, evidence, and alternative explanation.
3. **Original data exhibit** using de-identified observation, class data, structured self-observation, or supplied data.
4. **Intervention prototype** such as revised wording, sequence, disclosure, default, feedback, commitment, or coordination mechanism.
5. **Evaluation plan** with treatment, comparison, primary outcome, sample, uncertainty, ethics, and stopping rule.
6. **Implementation roadmap** with stakeholder, cost, risk, and unintended consequence.
7. **Professional presentation** suitable for a decision-maker audience.

## Constraints

Do not collect names, private records, protected characteristics, or covert recordings. Do not deploy an intervention involving other people without instructor approval. Observation and prototype design do not establish causal impact.
