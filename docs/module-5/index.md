## Learning outcomes

By the end of Module 5, students will be able to:

1. Explain availability, representativeness, base-rate neglect, and the law of small numbers.
2. Use natural frequencies to calculate posterior probability.
3. Distinguish the gambler's fallacy from hot-hand beliefs and genuine state dependence.
4. Separate overestimation, overplacement, and overprecision.
5. Explain how sampling, shot selection, learning, and calibration complicate streak analysis.
6. Diagnose cognitive, emotional, and social conditions that may affect judgment.
7. State a calibrated conclusion and identify evidence that would change it.

## Two-week pathway

**Week 9:** availability, representativeness, Bayesian updating, small samples, and random sequences.  
**Week 10:** hot-hand evidence, confidence calibration, decision states, class analysis, and the Judgment Audit.
